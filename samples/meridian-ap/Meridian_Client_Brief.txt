────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. CURRENT AP PROCESS (AS-IS)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Incoming supplier invoices arrive as PDF in ap-invoices@meridian-industrial.example (shared mailbox).
• Clerks download attachments, run OCR (ABBYY on desktop), correct line items manually.
• Matching in SAP: purchase order (PO), goods receipt (GR), invoice — classic 3-way match.
• Clean match: ~2–3 minutes per invoice. Exception: 15–20 minutes plus waiting on buyers.
• Volume: ~3,100 supplier invoices/month (excluding intercompany).
• Exception rate: ~26% require clarification (price variance, partial delivery, missing PO, duplicates).
• Credit notes (~5% of volume) handled manually; not in scope for initial automation.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. PAIN POINTS & TARGETS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Pain:
  – Month-end close target is 5 business days (CFO priority); AP is a bottleneck.
  – Buyers are interrupted daily for match clarifications.
  – Duplicate postings occurred twice in 2025 (caught post-payment; remediation costly).

Targets for pilot (invoice matching only):
  – Auto-match rate: from ~0% assisted today to ≥85% on “clean” rule set.
  – Manual effort on automatable core: from ~75 h/month to ≤12 h/month.
  – Zero wrong postings outside agreed tolerances.
  – 100% duplicate detection before posting.
  – Cycle time for clean cases: under 30 minutes mailbox-to-posting.

Loaded cost assumption for business case: EUR 45/hour (fully loaded clerk).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. MATCHING RULES (BUSINESS SUMMARY)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
• Quantity: exact match PO line vs GR vs invoice line.
• Unit price: exact unless active discount agreement in SAP.
• Total amount: rounding tolerance up to EUR 0.50.
• If deviation vs PO >2% → always hold for human review.
• Invoice number already posted → block duplicate; never auto-post.
• Invoices > EUR 5,000 → one-click human approval even if rules pass.

Out of scope for phase 1: credit notes, warehouse labelling, supplier self-service portal.
