PRE-MEETING PITCH (PPT #1) - "Ai Tech Borek Presentation" deck

This deck is sent before the first meeting. It is a discussion basis, not an offer and not a commitment.

STRUCTURE
- At most 8 slides in total, cover and closing included. Fewer is better than padded.
- Order: cover -> who_we_are (fixed, always slide 2) -> 3 to 5 content slides built from the approved Discovery pages -> closing (always last).
- Pick the layouts that fit the content; a layout may be repeated, but do not pad. Good fits: contrast, four_cards, process, phased,
  pillars, matrix_notes, faq, flow_facts, big_number. Do NOT use packages, price_tables or options (they carry prices).
- Design: follow the look of the first Borek deck. Every content slide is a designed layout (cards, columns, steps), never a plain
  text list. Use `matrix_notes` only when nothing else fits. Preferred recipe for the 3 to 5 content slides:
    client context + opportunity -> contrast (left = what we know, right = what we want to explore, 3-4 short bullets each)
    Borek approach               -> pillars (3-5 cards, n "01".. , short title, one sentence) or process (3 stages)
    relevant use case            -> four_cards (omit strip.items only if no approved fact fits) or contrast
    pilot proposal               -> phased (3-4 steps, when = "Step 1".., short title, one sentence)
    next steps                   -> process (3 stages) or flow_facts
  Every card has a title of 1-4 words and one sentence of at most 20 words. Always fill `lead` (one sentence) and the bottom
  `panel` / `statement` (kicker + one sentence) so no band stays empty. Titles are statements of at most 8 words.
- Cover: kicker "Borek AI Tech · <the meeting topic from the Discovery cover>", title = the client's situation in two short lines
  (use "\n" between the lines), intro = one short paragraph about the topic of the meeting.

CONTENT
- The approved Discovery pages are the ONLY source for facts about the client. A page marked "(no approved content - do not invent any)"
  has nothing to say: leave that topic out or phrase it as an open question for the meeting. Unknown company facts stay unknown.
- Borek's own wording (who we are, products, security, locations) may come from the fixed Borek facts above.
- Follow the Discovery narrative: client context -> opportunity -> Borek approach -> relevant use case -> pilot proposal -> next steps.
- Impact statements are industry benchmarks or working hypotheses, never customer commitments.
- NO prices, price ranges, currency amounts (EUR, euro, $, USD ...), ROI, return on investment, or cost / budget / savings figures anywhere.
  Ignore the price facts in the fixed Borek facts. Leave every "price" field out. Write "scope and terms to be agreed in the meeting" when needed.
- Never use the words "price", "pricing", "fixed-price", "cost", "budget", "savings" or "ROI" in any slide text, and do not describe
  commercial or engagement models (fixed-price projects, dedicated teams, licence models). Do not copy such wording from the fixed
  Borek facts; describe what Borek does, not how it is billed. Use "pilot" or "project" instead of "fixed-price project".
- Next steps are proposals for the meeting, not commitments.

SOURCES
- Every slide except who_we_are and closing carries "sources": a JSON array of the reference ids it draws on, written exactly as they
  appear in the block headers of the source material (for example ["discovery.opportunity"]). Never invent an id.
